Living PRD (Product Requirements Document)

Updated every week as we test with real users and learn what works.

1. Target User & The Job to Be Done

Who is this for? Undergraduate and graduate students who are recruiting. First reachable users: my NYU Stern MBA classmates.

The painful "Before" workflow: Recruiting is overwhelming. Cold applying on LinkedIn rarely works anymore, so networking is now crucial. But preparing for each coffee chat or event takes 30+ minutes of scattered research, and contacts and follow-ups get lost in notes.

The "After" workflow with this product: The student sets career goals and target companies once and connects Google Calendar. For each networking event they get a 2-page prep brief, generated 48 hours before and emailed 24 hours before.

2. Scope & Explicit Non-Goals

  • In Scope (Core Workflow, v1): Profile (career goals + target companies) → Google Calendar events → 2-page prep brief → chat to ask follow-up questions or add info to the brief.
  • Out of Scope #1: No scraping LinkedIn or building profiles of people from web data.
  • Out of Scope #2: No job search or application tracking.
  • Out of Scope #3: No CV or keyword analysis.

What the 2-page prep brief contains (with links in every section):

  • Event, date, time and location
  • Person and role
  • Company overview
  • Relevant products
  • Latest financial results and highlights (if public)
  • 5 relevant company news items from the last 3 months
  • 5 relevant industry news items and why they matter
  • 10 suggested questions

Roadmap (after the core workflow):

  • Contacts list with company, role and last contact date.
  • Follow-up drafts (thank-you notes, to-dos).
  • AI notetaker integration (e.g. Granola) to keep a history of each conversation.
  • Industry news tab.
  • Recommended events (conferences, panels, recruiting events) based on the student's goals and target companies.
  • Recommended contacts based on materials the student uploads (event attendee lists, alumni directory exports), plus suggested roles to look for at each target company, with drafts to start the conversation.
